🔹 Part 1: Radians & Angular Displacement (Topics 1-2)

  1. 1. Convert 120° to radians:
    ✅ Answer: b) 2π/3 rad
  2. 2. If an object rotates clockwise, the angular displacement is:
    ✅ Answer: b) Negative
  3. 3. The arc length of a circle with radius 2 m and central angle π/3 rad is:
    ✅ Answer: b) 2π/3 m (x = rθ = 2 × π/3)

🔹 Part 2: Angular & Linear Velocity (Topics 3-4)

  1. 4. A rotating toy makes one complete counterclockwise rotation in 1 minute. What is its angular velocity?
    ✅ Answer: b) 2π rad/min
  2. 5. The relationship between linear velocity (v) and angular velocity (ω) is:
    ✅ Answer: b) v = rω
  3. 6. A CD-ROM has linear velocity 1.4 m/s at radius 0.027 m. What is the angular velocity?
    ✅ Answer: b) 51.9 rad/s (ω = v/r = 1.4/0.027)

🔹 Part 3: Angular Acceleration (Topic 5)

  1. 7. A clothes washer drum rotates at 635 rev/min and stops in 8.0 s. What is the angular acceleration?
    ✅ Answer: b) -8.31 rad/s² (negative = slowing down)
  2. 8. Angular acceleration (α) is defined as:
    ✅ Answer: b) Δω/Δt

🔹 Part 4: Torque (Topics 6-8)

  1. 9. The formula for torque when force is applied at an angle θ is:
    ✅ Answer: b) τ = Frsinθ
  2. 10. Maximum torque occurs when the angle θ is:
    ✅ Answer: c) 90° (sin90° = 1)
  3. 11. A torque of 55.0 N·m requires a force of 135 N. What is the lever arm?
    ✅ Answer: b) 0.407 m (r = τ/F = 55/135)
  4. 12. Counterclockwise torque is considered:
    ✅ Answer: a) Positive

🔹 Part 5: Moment of Inertia (Topics 9-11)

  1. 13. Moment of inertia (I) for a single point mass is:
    ✅ Answer: b) I = mr²
  2. 14. If the distance from the axis doubles, the moment of inertia becomes:
    ✅ Answer: b) 4 times bigger (I ∝ r²)
  3. 15. Which object has the largest moment of inertia (same mass & radius)?
    ✅ Answer: b) Thin hoop (I = mr²)

🔹 Part 6: Newton's 2nd Law for Rotation (Topic 12)

  1. 16. Newton's second law for rotational motion is:
    ✅ Answer: b) τnet = Iα
  2. 17. A pulley with radius 0.150 m has a force of 13.0 N applied. What is the torque?
    ✅ Answer: b) 1.95 N·m (τ = Fr = 13.0 × 0.150)

🔹 Part 7: Center of Mass & Equilibrium (Topics 13-14)

  1. 18. The center of mass of an object:
    ✅ Answer: b) Can be in a place where there is no actual mass
  2. 19. For static equilibrium, which conditions must be met?
    ✅ Answer: c) Both ΣF = 0 and Στ = 0

🔹 Part 8: Impulse & Linear Momentum (Topics 15-19)

  1. 20. Impulse (J) is equal to:
    ✅ Answer: b) FΔt = Δp
  2. 21. Linear momentum (p) is defined as:
    ✅ Answer: b) mv
  3. 22. A 2200 kg vehicle traveling at 26 m/s stops. What is the change in momentum?
    ✅ Answer: b) -5.7×10⁴ kg·m/s (Δp = 0 - 57200)

🔹 Part 9: Angular Momentum (Topics 20-21)

  1. 23. Angular momentum (L) is defined as:
    ✅ Answer: b) L = Iω
  2. 24. An ice skater has I = 0.50 kg·m² and ω = 2.5 rotations/s. What is L?
    ✅ Answer: b) 7.9 kg·m²/s (L = Iω = 0.50 × 2.5 × 2π)

🔹 Part 10: Conservation of Momentum (Topics 22-25)

  1. 25. When an ice skater pulls arms in during a spin, what happens?
    ✅ Answer: b) Moment of inertia decreases, speed increases (conservation of L)
